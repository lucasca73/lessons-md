## The Color Wheel

Primary Colors - Red, Blue and Yellow
Secondary Colors - Orange, Purple, Green

Hue - True form of the colors
Value - Brightness, amount of white or black
Saturation - Vividness

## Warm and Cool Colors

In general Warmer colors will stand out more. So it is nice to have it on Foreground and in the Background to use cooler tones.
It follows similar how the atmosphere works in real life, as far way it gets cooler and desaturated. 

On this exercise we choose the colors from a cool bright color in the background to a warm dark color for the foreground.
In addition to add to the landscape a foggy was added at the bottom of each layer.

## Color Psychology

(Standout) Red - Passion, Danger, Strength
Orange - Youth, modern, Energict, playfull
Yellow - Cheerfull, Sponteneous, Hope, Happines
Green - Stability, Healthy, Prosperity
Blue - Cool and Calm. Melancoly. Relaxation, Trustworthy
Purple - Elegance, Loyalty, Luxury, Fantasy, Wealth
