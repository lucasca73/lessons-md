## First Lesson is `Your First Animation`

Let's draw circles from LEFT to RIGHT in 12 drawings.

Prof. Benjamin introduces two concepts in the lesson:
- Anticipation
- Overshoot

``- Anticipation is the preparation of an action to increase its strength.``

``- Overshoot makes a stronger action by adding the recovery frames at the end.``
