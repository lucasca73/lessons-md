# Introduction to Digital Coloring

In this Course Feefal (Linnea Kikuchi), guides us through mixing graphite and digital art by drawing with a pencil first, taking a picture and coloring with a tablet. She used Procreate and an iPad. I am using Krita and Wacom Tablet.

The outcome didn't came as much as expected. I think the process that Feefal proposes were to focused on Procreate so I felt missing the potential as I am using another tool and I don't know exactly how to perform the same.

Although, there was quite a time that I didn't draw using pencil and coloring digitally is interesting. I followed her proposal to make the draw image a multiply layer.
But I ended up with a washed color, I also think I didn't choose a nice color pallete. Maybe I could learn it another lesson.

I didn't work in the lines as well because I wanted to preserve the graphite as this was the initial intention I got from the course. Looking at it in the end a line could really help pop and make things clearer.

The step of rendering is something that doesn't get into my head as well, my drawings looks pretty rough.
